## Concrete datasets and model results

The KiTS19 manifest is `_derived/datasets/kits19-segmentation/manifest.json`, dataset version `455cfd7c06b0a16e608b510704e19226da325a82729425b8c1a696c05ff575dd`. It contains 210 matched CT/mask pairs. Its CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 data terms are recorded for local non-commercial research, not commercial release.

**The 20-epoch KiTS19 experiment failed its tumour target.** In the frozen 32-patient internal test, kidney Dice was **0.8329** (patient bootstrap 95% interval 0.8039–0.8578), renal-tumour Dice **0.0000** across all 32 positive-reference cases, and mean case macro Dice **0.4165**. The checkpoint SHA-256 is `9acfa4f810ff7eb7be0e35c9ff465f1dee9385f7dc4ce9141ee54ae6e58651a2`. It is retained as failed research evidence and blocked from upload use; a zero tumour mask must not imply that no tumour exists. Further development needs a reviewed foreground/class-sampling and loss design, longer development experiments selected on validation, resolution checks for small tumours, and a fresh independent final evaluation. This exposed test cannot become a repeatedly tuned selection set.

The LIDC manifest is `_derived/datasets/lidc-nodule-segmentation/manifest.json`, dataset version `03d3eb76af2f2d4f1f83966d285b90c1fc33a510352974d94871e8bdfed9ec12`. It contains **299 patients: 209 train, 45 validation, 45 test**, with no patient overlap. All retained series have four reader sessions; a mask voxel requires at least two readers. **LIDC-IDRI-0017 is excluded** because ten outlines reference SOP instances absent from its selected series. This subset covers reader-outlined nodules of at least 3 mm, not every lung abnormality. Sources and research authorization are recorded in `dataset_registry/tasks.json`.

The active downloader's JSON still retains an earlier LIDC-IDRI-0295 transfer error; that archive was repaired and verified separately (`artifacts/audits/lidc-range-repair-2026-11-09.json`) and is included in the inspected manifest. The finalizer reconciles such old failure records after obtaining the download lock; it does not overwrite the running worker's status concurrently.

**CQ500 source blocker:** a fresh complete-sized download of CQ500-CT-120 is byte-identical to the preserved earlier copy, matches the source object's recorded MD5 metadata, and still lacks a ZIP end-of-central-directory record. The source file itself appears incomplete. It remains refused rather than counted as verified. See `artifacts/audits/cq500-source-invalid-2026-11-09.json`. A readable publisher replacement or separately reviewed complete copy is needed; repeated resume commands cannot fix these bytes. No corrupt medical scan has been silently repaired, filled in or treated as normal.

These six-class models use frozen ImageNet ResNet18 features and training-only feature/metadata preprocessing. Regularization and temperature were selected on validation only. Serving parity checks reproduced six saved predictions per model (12 model/image checks), not every test prediction. The image-only temperature did not improve ECE (0.0941 before scaling); calibration is not guaranteed to improve every metric. Only **five melanoma images** are present in the test split, so rare-class performance is unstable. The observed fusion improvement is descriptive, not evidence of general clinical superiority. Age, gender and anatomical region may encode source-population bias. PAD-UFES-20 is clinical photography, not dermoscopy or pathology.

The colour check uses a floor selected only from training images. It refused none of the 304 skin test images and all 100 sampled images in each tested angiography/CT/radiograph family. The separate feature gate refused about 0.66% of skin test images. Cross-family tests have known gaps: these checks cannot prove image modality or exclude every inappropriate input. See `docs/validation/models/skin_padufes_input_quality.json` and the two `.ood.json` reports.
